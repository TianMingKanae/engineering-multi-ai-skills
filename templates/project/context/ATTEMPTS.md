# Record of attempts

Append every round attempt (pass, partial or fail). Keep failing objections explicit so later rounds and isolated briefings do not repeat known defects.

| Round | ID | Direction | Author | Method | Artifact / Revision | Single check | Cross check | Outcome | Failing objection / Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Rules:
- `Outcome` reaches `pass` only when both `Single check` and `Cross check` pass under reviewers distinct from the attempt author.
- Any `fail` or `partial` attempt must record the exact failing objection and artifact path.
- If a direction stalls for two or more failed rounds on the same objection, re-dispatch literature search (`P`) before launching another attempt.
