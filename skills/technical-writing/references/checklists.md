# Revision passes

Use the checks relevant to the requested document. The checklist is a review aid, not a new approval chain. Tick an item only after inspecting the affected artifact and evidence.

## Purpose and evidence

- [ ] Audience, purpose, scope and destination style are clear.
- [ ] Each claim has a source or is explicitly identified as unresolved/proposed.
- [ ] Values, units, comparison conditions, uncertainty and exclusions match the evidence.
- [ ] Negative findings and limitations needed to judge the work remain visible.
- [ ] Methods describe sufficient inputs, settings and stopping rules for the stated reproducibility claim.

## Structure and language

- [ ] Title, abstract, contribution list and conclusion agree with the actual scope.
- [ ] The introduction identifies the problem, relevant prior work, gap and purpose.
- [ ] Observations, interpretations and recommendations remain distinguishable.
- [ ] Paragraphs develop a point with relevant support; terminology and pronouns are consistent.
- [ ] Voice, modifiers, comparisons and list structure preserve meaning.
- [ ] Filler is removed without replacing precise technical terms with weaker synonyms.
- [ ] Articles, tense, agreement, punctuation, symbols and references were checked.

## Graphics and delivery

- [ ] Figures/tables show the intended inventory with truthful axes, units and uncertainty.
- [ ] Captions are self-contained and labels remain readable at the destination size.
- [ ] Necessary supplementary detail is cross-referenced; material qualifications are not hidden.
- [ ] Citations identify verified sources; no invented bibliography entry or unsupported quote was added.
- [ ] Coauthor ownership and the project's existing review workflow are respected.
- [ ] Rendered layout was checked when formatting changed.
- [ ] Reviewer responses describe changes/experiments that actually exist.
- [ ] Private paths, internal routing and working placeholders are absent from the deliverable.
